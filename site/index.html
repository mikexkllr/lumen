How it works

Microlearning that sticks

Lessons take a few minutes and start with a problem, not a lecture: 31 kinds of interactive problems – drag, build, simulate, play against the computer – instead of videos and walls of text.

1

Try it first

You get your hands on the idea right away: fill, sort, program a robot, tune a simulation. Explanations come when they help – one small step at a time.

2

Instant, targeted feedback

Lumen recognises typical mistakes and tells you what went wrong – then gives you a second try. Each lesson goes deeper in three stages: core, deeper, expert.

3

Remember it for good

Spaced repetition (FSRS) brings problems back right before you forget them – with new numbers every time. Daily puzzle, streaks and XP keep you going.

Meet Lux

A tutor that asks before it tells

Lux is in every lesson. Stuck? Lux gives a hint. Wrong? Lux first asks how you got your answer and builds on your reasoning. A built-in guard makes sure Lux never just gives the solution away.

  • Hints, examples and “explain it simpler” on every screen
  • Mistakes review at the end of every lesson
  • Explain it to Lux – teaching is the best way to learn
I filled 3 parts. Isn’t that 75 %?
LuxGood start! The bar has 8 parts. How many percent is one part worth?
Oh – 12.5 %. So I need 6!
LuxExactly. 6 × 12.5 % = 75 %. ✨
Open & private
